3 Get ready to ship

E-bike must-dos

  1. Drop the battery charge below 30% before you ship.
  2. Power the battery off and make sure it can't switch on in transit.
  3. Pad heavily and use the Bike Box Large, which is rated for e-bikes.
  4. Remove or fully cover every Class 9 lithium-ion hazmat marking.
  5. Ships ground only, in the contiguous US — over 70 lb, add a heavy-package sticker.

For the shipping form

  • Ship-from and ship-to street addresses (no PO boxes).
  • Your box measured at its widest points — length, width and height, each rounded up to the next inch.
  • The packed weight, rounded up to the next pound.
  • Your bike's market value, for the declared value and optional Premium Protection.
  • The reason for the shipment: buy/sell/gift, warranty/return/repair, vacation/event, or move.

Good to know

  • Every bike needs some disassembly — pedals, wheels, bars and seat come off, even in a box.
  • Loose batteries and CO₂ cartridges can't ship — an e-bike battery must stay installed in the bike.
  • Drop off at a UPS Store and keep the receipt, or schedule a pickup — you don't need to be home.
  • Premium Protection covers loss or damage up to your declared value; add it when you book.
  • Shipping internationally? Expect duties and taxes, and possibly a tax ID.

How shipping works

  1. Buy and build your box, then pack your bike.
  2. Enter your addresses, box size and weight to get the price, then buy your label.
  3. Schedule a pickup or drop it at a UPS Store — keep the receipt.
  4. Track it from pickup to delivery.

Common bike-shipping questions
What size bike box do I need?
BikeFlights sells four boxes. The Bike Box Small (43 × 10 × 24 in) fits kids', BMX bikes and framesets; the Bike Box Medium (45 × 12 × 30 in) fits most road, gravel and smaller mountain bikes; the Bike Box Large (64 × 14 × 32 in) keeps the rear wheel on and is rated for road, gravel, mountain and e-bikes; the Bike Box Extra Large (56 × 21 × 32 in) fits aero road, triathlon and fat bikes. Shop them at BikeFlights Bike Boxes and check measuring on BikeFlights Dimensions & Rates.
Do I have to take my bike apart to ship it?
Yes — every bike needs some disassembly. You typically remove the pedals, wheels, handlebars and seat/seatpost and pad everything well, even in a bike box. Step-by-step help is on the BikeFlights packing guides.
Who packs my bike — can a shop do it?
You can pack it yourself using the guides and videos, or have a local shop or mobile mechanic pack it for you. Find one with the BikeFlights Bike Shop Finder.
How do I ship an e-bike?
An e-bike ships with its battery installed under the UN 3171 classification — it is not treated as hazmat. Lower the charge below 30%, power the battery off, pack it in the Bike Box Large, remove or fully cover all Class 9 lithium-ion hazmat markings, and ship ground within the contiguous US. Full details on BikeFlights E-bike Shipping.
Can I use my own box or case?
Yes — you can ship in your own box or hard case. Measure it at its widest points and round every dimension up to the next inch, and the weight up to the next pound. See BikeFlights Dimensions & Rates.
How does BikeFlights shipping work?
Pack your bike, book your shipment (enter addresses, box size and weight to get the price and buy a label), schedule a pickup or drop it at a UPS Store, then track it to delivery. Start at BikeFlights Shipping.
What can't I ship?
Loose or standalone batteries and CO₂ cartridges can't ship, and you can't ship to a PO box — use a street address. An e-bike battery must stay installed in the bike. See the BikeFlights FAQ.
